Who We Are

FurnishIQ is a Saudi design-build company specializing in interior design, turnkey fit-out, furniture solutions, and project management. We create exceptional environments across residential, hospitality, commercial, and government sectors — transforming concepts into functional, inspiring, and enduring spaces.

From concept development and engineering to procurement, execution, and handover, our multidisciplinary team delivers seamless project experiences through innovation, quality craftsmanship, and disciplined project management.

Our Story

Our journey began with a simple observation: while the market offered many design and construction solutions, there remained a significant gap for discerning clients seeking spaces that truly reflected their individuality, lifestyle, and aspirations.

FurnishIQ was established to close that gap — delivering personalized, high-quality environments through a fully integrated design, build, and management approach.

Part of Arsan Global

FurnishIQ is the Design, Build, and Management subsidiary of Arsan Global, a diversified international holding group headquartered in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and established in 2007. With a growing presence across the Middle East, Europe, North America, and Asia, Arsan Global operates through specialized companies in logistics, technology, real estate, trading, business consulting, and integrated project solutions.

Backed by the financial strength, international network, and multidisciplinary expertise of Arsan Global, FurnishIQ combines creative vision, engineering excellence, and disciplined execution to create exceptional spaces that inspire, perform, and endure.
