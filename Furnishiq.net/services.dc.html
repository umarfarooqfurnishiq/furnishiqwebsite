Our Process

A Proven Process, Refined on Every Project

At FurnishIQ, our commitment to excellence extends to a proven, design-build process built around clarity at every stage — from concept and material selection through fit-out and final handover. This structured approach lets us take full ownership of a project's outcome, protecting quality, schedule, and budget in equal measure, whichever service leads the engagement.

Dependable Warranty

Our commitment goes beyond handover. Every FurnishIQ project is backed by a standard one-year materials and workmanship warranty, with extended cover available on request — closing any gaps left by manufacturer coverage. It's the same structured accountability that runs through our entire process, so your investment stays protected long after the final walkthrough.
