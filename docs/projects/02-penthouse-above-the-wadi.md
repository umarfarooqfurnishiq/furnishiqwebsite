## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **The view as the plan.** Penthouse Above the Wadi is a FurnishIQ design concept for a full-floor duplex at the top of a residential tower in west Riyadh. The 1,400 m² plan is organised around a single decision: the double-height living room faces Wadi Hanifa directly, and every principal space on the entertaining level opens toward it.
2. **Two levels, two tempos.** The lower level is for hosting — the 8-metre living room, a private majlis, dining for twelve, a show kitchen with a separate service kitchen, and a backlit onyx bar. The upper level is for the family: five suites, a master wing with a spa bath that looks across the wadi, and a private gym. A roof terrace with an infinity-edge pool completes the composition.
3. **Material in the light.** The palette is chosen for a home that is lit by the sky. Honey onyx is backlit so it glows at dusk; dark smoked oak and smoked bronze give the double-height volume weight; crema limestone floors carry the daylight deep into the plan. Ivory plaster keeps the upper level calm.
4. **The staircase.** A sculptural staircase in smoked oak treads on a folded bronze stringer connects the two levels inside the living room. It is detailed as a piece of furniture — no visible fixings, a continuous bronze handrail, and an integrated warm light line under every tread.
5. **Engineering at height.** Full-height glazing on a west-facing elevation demands a precise climate strategy. FurnishIQ plans concealed linear diffusers along the glazing line, motorised solar and blackout blinds recessed into the ceiling, acoustic separation between the two levels, and a single smart-home system controlling lighting scenes, shading and climate room by room. The pool and terrace services are coordinated with the tower's structure from the first design stage.
6. **Furniture for the view.** Seating is kept low so the view stays uninterrupted: a long sofa in sand bouclé, lounge chairs in cognac leather, and a dining table in smoked oak for twelve. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out across both levels and the terraces, MEP engineering, and furniture specification, sourcing and installation.
