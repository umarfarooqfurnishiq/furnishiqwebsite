## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **A building that keeps its counsel.** Family Office HQ is a FurnishIQ design concept for the private headquarters of a family office in west Riyadh. The 3,200 m² building presents a quiet limestone wall to the street and opens inward, around a courtyard with an olive tree and a long water rill.
2. **Arranged around the courtyard.** Every principal room faces the courtyard: the family council room, the principal's office, the library and a private dining room on the upper floor; a guest majlis, meeting rooms and the investment team studio below. A lower-ground level holds the archive and a secure records room.
3. **Najdi, reinterpreted.** The architecture draws on Najdi building: thick limestone walls with small triangular openings, athel timber beams across the ceilings, and deep reveals that keep the interiors cool and calm. Smoked oak joinery, dark bronze and tobacco suede bring the rooms to the scale of a private house rather than a corporate office.
4. **The council room.** The family council room seats sixteen around a single smoked oak table beneath athel beams, with acoustic lime plaster walls, concealed AV and a view into the courtyard. Next to it, the library combines reading tables with the family's archive, housed in floor-to-ceiling oak shelving with bronze ladders.
5. **Engineered for privacy.** Privacy and security shape the engineering. FurnishIQ plans acoustic separation between every meeting room, secure data and records rooms with dedicated cooling and fire suppression, displacement ventilation concealed in the reveals, and a single smart-building system for lighting, shading, climate and access control.
6. **Furniture for the long term.** Furniture is specified to last a generation: a council table in solid smoked oak, chairs in tobacco suede, library tables in oak and bronze, and hand-knotted wool rugs. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out across all three levels, MEP engineering, and furniture specification, sourcing and installation.
