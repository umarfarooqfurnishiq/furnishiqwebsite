## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **A house on the water's edge.** Obhur Beach Estate is a FurnishIQ design concept for a family estate on the Red Sea coast in north Jeddah. The 2,600 m² programme is divided across three buildings: a main house, a guest pavilion and a beach pavilion, all aligned on a single axis that runs from the entrance court to the shoreline.
2. **The great room.** At the centre of the main house, a sea-facing great room opens fully through retractable glazing onto a shaded terrace and the 30-metre pool. Dining for fourteen sits beneath a teak-boarded ceiling, with the family majlis and a show kitchen arranged behind it, away from the sun.
3. **Rawasheen, reinterpreted.** Jeddah's historic rawasheen — carved timber bay screens — are reinterpreted in hand-carved teak along the gallery that links the house. They filter the western light, give privacy from the beach, and let the sea breeze move through the plan.
4. **A coastal palette.** Coral stone, ivory lime plaster and sand travertine are chosen to stay calm and cool in a humid coastal climate. Bleached oak, natural linen and woven rattan keep the rooms light; aged brass is used sparingly, where the hand meets the house.
5. **Engineered for the coast.** Salt air and humidity shape every engineering decision. FurnishIQ plans dehumidification integrated with concealed linear air distribution, marine-grade fixings and hardware, corrosion-resistant MEP routing, and a single smart-home system controlling lighting, shading, climate and the retractable glazing room by room.
6. **Furniture for sea air.** Furniture is specified for the coast: performance linen upholstery, solid teak and rattan that weather gracefully, and low lounge pieces that keep the horizon in view. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out of all three buildings, MEP engineering, and furniture specification, sourcing and installation.
