## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **Arriving in the valley.** Desert Stone Resort is a FurnishIQ design concept for the public and guest spaces of a resort in AlUla. The 4,500 m² scope covers the arrival pavilion, the lobby lounge, a signature restaurant, a library lounge, a fire-pit terrace and the spa reception — the spaces every guest passes through.
2. **A sequence framed on the rock.** Guests arrive through a shaded court between rammed-earth walls, then step into a lobby lounge whose full-height glazing frames the sandstone formations outside. Each space in the sequence is oriented on a different view of the valley, so the landscape is revealed one frame at a time.
3. **Built from the valley.** The palette is taken from the site: rammed earth in the tones of the surrounding sand, local sandstone for floors and counters, and blackened steel for frames and screens. Handwoven wool in ochre and terracotta, dark oiled timber and aged brass keep the interiors warm against the scale of the landscape.
4. **The signature restaurant.** The restaurant seats 120 under a ceiling of dark timber beams, with an open kitchen behind a sandstone counter and a long glazed wall facing the rock formations at sunset. After dark, the fire-pit terrace outside becomes the resort's evening gathering place.
5. **Engineering in the desert.** A remote desert site with large temperature swings shapes the engineering. FurnishIQ plans thermal mass in the rammed-earth walls, low-level displacement cooling hidden in the joinery, solar shading tuned to each façade, dust-sealed glazing systems, and lighting kept low and warm to protect the night sky.
6. **Furniture made for the place.** Furniture is specified to belong to the landscape: low lounge seating in handwoven wool and leather, dining tables in solid dark oak, sandstone side tables and blackened-steel lamps. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out, MEP engineering, and furniture specification, sourcing and installation.
