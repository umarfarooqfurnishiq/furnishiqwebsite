## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **A jewel box on the street.** Maison de Joaillerie is a FurnishIQ design concept for a flagship jewellery house in Jeddah. The 900 m² building sits behind a perforated bronze screen that filters the street into a soft glow, giving privacy without closing the house off.
2. **The main salon.** Inside, a calm main salon is lined with a wall of slim vitrines set into ivory silk panels. A backlit rose onyx counter sits at its centre, and a sculptural spiral stair in champagne-gold metal and white marble leads to the upper level.
3. **Private salons.** Upstairs, two private salons and a bridal salon receive clients by appointment. Each has its own lounge seating in cream velvet, a viewing table in walnut and onyx, and controlled lighting for looking at stones in true colour.
4. **A palette of soft light.** The materials are chosen to flatter both the jewellery and the client: rose onyx lit from within, ivory silk on the walls, honed white marble underfoot, champagne-gold metal for frames and fittings, and cream velvet for seating.
5. **Engineering for value.** A jewellery house carries particular demands. FurnishIQ plans museum-grade vitrine lighting with high colour rendering, glass and locking systems to insurance standards, a secure vault and back-of-house with separate access, integrated CCTV and access control hidden in the joinery, and quiet, precise climate control for the private salons.
6. **Furniture made to measure.** Every vitrine, counter, viewing table and seat is designed for the house: vitrines in champagne-gold metal and low-iron glass, viewing tables in walnut and onyx, and lounge chairs in cream velvet. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out, MEP engineering, and bespoke furniture and display joinery.
