## Narrative (project page, 6 paragraphs)
1. **One headquarters, eight floors.** The Executive Floors is a FurnishIQ design concept for a corporate headquarters on eight upper floors of an office tower in north Riyadh. The 14,000 m² programme is organised vertically: arrival and client spaces at the top, executive offices below, and six open workplace floors connected by a feature stair.
2. **The sky lobby.** Visitors arrive in a double-height sky lobby with full-height glazing over the city. A monolithic travertine reception desk sits beneath a slatted walnut ceiling; a client majlis and meeting suites open directly off the lobby, so guests never enter the working floors.
3. **The executive level.** The boardroom seats twenty-four at a single book-matched walnut table, with acoustic walls, concealed AV and a view along the city skyline. The chairman's office and executive suites share a private reception, a dining room and their own vertical connection to the sky lobby.
4. **A ceiling that does the work.** The slatted walnut ceiling is the project's organising element. Linear lighting, air distribution, sprinklers, speakers and sensors are coordinated into the gaps between slats, so the ceiling reads as one continuous plane from the lobby to the workplace floors.
5. **Engineered for 900 people.** The workplace floors are planned for 900 staff, with zoned air handling, demand-controlled ventilation, acoustic separation between focus and collaboration areas, and a building management system that controls lighting, shading and climate floor by floor. Prayer rooms, ablution areas and a staff café are provided on every floor.
6. **Furniture as part of the fit-out.** Workstations, executive desks, boardroom table and lounge pieces are specified in the same walnut, bronze and cognac leather as the architecture. FurnishIQ's scope covers the complete design-build — interior design and photoreal 3D visualisation, fit-out across all eight floors, MEP engineering, and furniture specification, sourcing and installation.
